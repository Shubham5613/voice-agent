# What a credit-card telecaller in India knows before dialling

Compiled 25 Oct 2026 from public sources (web search + fetch). Source URL after every claim; unverified items listed at the end.

## 1. Banks calling their own customers (ETB)

**Scale.** HDFC Bank's CFO told analysts in Oct 2025 that "normally, it has been between 65%-75% or so is existing" for new card acquisitions, with "no big change" recently (https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2025-10-18-hdfc-bank-limited-stock). SBI Card sourced ~50% of its 0.94 mn Q2 FY26 new accounts via the SBI "banca" channel (SBI account holders), the rest open-market (https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/cut-in-earnings-expectations-valuations-to-cap-upside-in-sbi-cards-125102701176_1.html).

**Mechanics (primary source).** Axis Bank's board-approved Credit Card Issuance and Conduct Policy FY24-25 states the bank "sources credit card across ETB and NTB segments", runs "pre-qualification of ETB customers" for straight-through processing, and that telemarketing covers "inbound and outbound call-centers, wherein application can be completed real-time basis authentication for ETB customers". Portfolio offers are targeted using "attributes basis customer's banking, bureau & behavior on card accounts"; underwriting checks "bureau profile, limits enjoyed by the customer from other entities ... de-dupe against existing cardholders/negative list" (https://www.axis.bank.in/docs/default-source/comprehensive-notice-board/important-notices/bank's-policies/credit-card-issuance-and-conduct-policy.pdf). ICICI says pre-approved offers go to customers after it "assesses your credit history, income and relationship with the bank", visible under iMobile > Offers and NetBanking > Cards and Loans (https://www.icici.bank.in/personal-banking/cards/credit-card/pre-approved-credit-card). HDFC says existing customers check offers in NetBanking/MobileBanking; its pre-approved personal-loan page lists "healthy balances and saving habits" and "stable banking relationship" as triggers (https://www.hdfcbank.com/personal/resources/learning-centre/borrow/pre-approved-personal-loan-offers). Could not verify an HDFC statement that specifically names salary credits for card pre-approval.

**Bulk bureau pulls on existing customers.** BOB Financial/TransUnion CIBIL (2018) describe a tri-partite deal where CIBIL "implement[s] the credit policy and proprietary credit score based on ... CreditVision variables" over Bank of Baroda's own retail base to generate pre-approved cards (https://newsroom.transunioncibil.com/bob-financial-solutions-limited-to-offer-pre-approved-credit-cards-to-bank-of-baroda-customers/). CIC Regulations 2006, Reg. 9 permits a specified user to obtain credit information "to review and evaluate risk of its customers" and for "review or renewal or enhancement" of an existing facility (https://www.crifhighmark.com/media/3196/cic_rules_and_regulations.pdf), which is the legal hook for portfolio review on existing customers. Paisabazaar lists "routine account monitoring by your existing bank" as a soft enquiry (https://www.paisabazaar.com/credit-score/soft-inquiry-vs-hard-inquiry-aio/). No RBI text was found that explicitly addresses marketing-only bulk pulls.

## 2. Bureau access rules

**Who may pull, for what.** CICRA s.17 restricts CIC data to members and specified users (https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=9485). Reg. 9 lists the purposes: effective credit decisions, deterring serial defaulters, reviewing risk of its customers, judging creditworthiness of a borrower/client, and self-access. Marketing or prospecting of non-customers is not on the list (https://www.crifhighmark.com/media/3196/cic_rules_and_regulations.pdf). Reg. 3 adds insurers, telcos, CRAs, brokers as specified users; a 2021 amendment widened it to entities processing information "for the support or benefit of credit institutions" (https://law.asia/specified-users-cicra/).

**Consent / soft pull for marketing to non-customers.** RBI's Jan 2025 CIC direction says sharing with non-specified users (fintech apps) must rest on "prior written consent of the individual by any documented means", used "only for the agreed-upon purpose", deleted within six months, and CICs must SMS/email the borrower when a specified user accesses the CIR (https://www.medianama.com/2025/01/223-rbi-mandates-stricter-norms-credit-information-sharing/). The Nov 2025 CIC Directions tighten specified-user eligibility and audit of consent-based sharing (https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12929). No legal basis was found for a consent-less "soft pull" on a non-customer in India; the US-style "firm offer" prescreen exception does not exist in CICRA.

**RBI Card Directions, 28 Nov 2025 (RBI/DOR/2025-26/155).** Para 11(8): "written consent of the applicant shall be required before issuing a credit card". Para 12(1): issuer must "independently assess the credit risk"; 12(2): limits must consider limits with other entities "based on self-declaration or credit information from a CIC". Para 11(7): no reporting to CICs before activation. Para 11(1): rejection reasons must be conveyed in writing. Para 11(9): telemarketers must follow TRAI rules/NCPR, call only 10:00-19:00. Para 86: no disclosure of customer information without explicit consent naming purpose and recipients (https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=13155). Reg. 10(c) of the 2006 CIC Regulations separately requires written intimation within 30 days when credit is denied on the basis of a CIR (https://www.crifhighmark.com/media/3196/cic_rules_and_regulations.pdf).

**TransUnion CIBIL products.** Business pages returned 403; the "customer acquisition" page is at https://www.transunioncibil.com/solution/customer-acquisition. On the consumer side, CIBIL's "Personalized Loan Offers" show subscribers card/loan offers "tailored using the CIBIL Score, income, and other parameters", and "the lender alone decides whether to extend an offer" (https://www.cibil.com/faq/apply-for-a-loan). A product literally called "CIBIL Prospect" could not be verified.

**DPDP.** The DPDP Act's s.7 legitimate uses do not include marketing; s.7(a) covers only "the specified purpose for which the Data Principal has voluntarily provided her personal data" (https://www.dpdpa.com/dpdpa2023/chapter-2/section7.html). Rules notified 13/14 Nov 2025; consent provisions (ss.3-5, 6, 7-10) commence 13 May 2027 (https://www.livelaw.in/articles/dpdpa-says-about-call-centre-data-548760). TRAI's Feb 2025 TCCCPR amendment mandates 140-series numbers for promotional calls and limits inferred consent to the contractual relationship (https://securiti.ai/india-spam-rules-trai-latest-amendment/).

## 3. Marketplaces and lead generation

**Consent given.** Paisabazaar's bureau forms appoint it "authorised representative" to receive the CIR monthly, and its privacy policy allows sharing with "Business Partners (Banks/NBFCs)" (https://paisabazaar.com/privacy-policy; https://www.paisabazaar.com/frontview/terms_of_use_cibil.php/). OneCard's Experian consent is the same structure: OneCard as authorised representative, six months, for "credit card offerings based on the credit worthiness derived from the bureau data", no resale (https://www.getonecard.app/legal/experian_tnc/).

**Consumer reports.** Inventiva (31 Jul 2023): "As my credit score got up the number of calls increased"; another caller "forwarded my number to other bankers" (https://www.inventiva.co.in/?p=374065). ConsumerComplaints hosts an Oct 2025 complaint that details were shared with several banks after a score check (https://www.consumercomplaints.in/paisabazaarcom-b115696). A TechnoFino user got pre-approval SMS after deleting OneScore (https://technofino.in/community/threads/currently-holding-two-onecards.1347/latest). CRED/Paytm consent screens could not be retrieved.

**DSA leads.** The IBA Model Code of Conduct (adopted by CSB, 17 Jul 2025) permits calling a prospect only if they expressed interest, were referred, are an existing customer who consented, or their number "has been taken from one of the lists/directories/databases approved by the DSA Manager/Team leader", with "no calling on lists unless list is cleared by team leader" (https://www.csb.bank.in/pdf/model-code-of-conduct-17072025.pdf). The code thus formally sanctions purchased/approved databases.

**Leak evidence.** Aug 2025: 18 arrested after Teleperformance (Gurugram) CPP call-centre insiders leaked SBI Card customer data (https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/data-leak-from-gurugram-call-centre-leads-to-rs-2-60-cr-credit-card-fraud-18-nabbed). Mar 2023: Cyberabad police bust a Noida-run gang selling data of 16.8 crore people via Justdial, including "credit and debit card holders (AXIS, HSBC)" (https://www.medianama.com/2023/03/223-hyderabad-police-personal-data-16-crore-citizens-sold/). A Noida private-bank card-department data-entry operator leaked new-cardholder details (https://m.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/cyber-fraud-gang-busted-11-held-438832). LocalCircles: 56% blame banks/financial firms for data in the public domain (https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/87-of-people-say-personal-data-compromised-in-public-domain-survey-125030300919_1.html); 34% of account holders were pitched accounts they already hold (https://www.moneylife.in/article/6-in-10-citizens-surveyed-report-personal-data-breach-by-loan-service-providers-localcircles/68922.html). Truecaller: 4,168 crore spam calls in India in 2025 (https://deccanherald.com/technology/truecaller-report-india-hit-by-4168-crore-spam-calls-in-2025-3896311).

## 4. Card-on-card

**Why they ask.** Card-for-card is an underwriting "surrogate": "banks issue new credit cards based on the customer's existing credit card. The customer's card statement is used for assessing their creditworthiness", alongside payroll and AQB surrogates (https://m2pfintech.com/blog/solving-for-ntbs-credit-card-issuance-challenges/). RBI Para 12(2) also requires issuers to consider "limits enjoyed from other entities", so the limit answer feeds both eligibility and limit-setting (https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=13155). A high existing limit signals an already-underwritten customer; the telecaller is qualifying the lead.

**Rules.** No bank page publishes card-on-card thresholds; this could not be verified for HDFC, Axis, SBI Card, ICICI or IDFC. Forum evidence (DesiDime, ~2018): card "min 6 months old but most banks ask for 1 year", latest statement plus front-of-card copy, "you can apply all except Amex provided you have threshold limit"; HDFC/Axis reportedly did not do card-on-card then, contested later (https://www.desidime.com/discussions/card-on-card-basis-credit-card). SBI Card's documented income-waiver route is for home-loan (Rs 10 lakh+) or auto-loan (Rs 3 lakh+) customers, not card-on-card (https://moneylife.in/article/services-sbi-cards-initiatives/24603.html). Minimum-limit figures (Rs 50,000 / 1 lakh) are DSA folklore; unverified.

## 6. Verdict: arrows in the dark or data-driven?

| Caller | Typically knows | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Bank calling its own customer | Name, phone, city, account balances/transaction behaviour, salary credits if salary account, internal score, bureau score and other-bank limits (portfolio review), pre-computed offer and limit | High: Axis policy, HDFC 65-75% ETB, ICICI/HDFC offer pages, BOB-CIBIL deal |
| Bank/DSA calling a non-customer | Name, phone, often city; sometimes employer/"salaried" tag from bought/approved lists or marketplace leads; no lawful bureau score until consent; hence the "do you have another card, what limit?" qualifying question | Medium: IBA code sanctions approved databases; leak cases; forum card-on-card practice; thresholds unverified |
| Fintech (OneCard, Scapia, Kiwi, Jupiter) | Only what the user gave in-app: KYC, consented bureau report, AA bank data; pre-approval SMS to its own app base | Medium-high on mechanics; cold-calling of non-users unverified |

Bottom line: a bank calling its own customer is heavily data-driven. An open-market DSA call is semi-blind: a list with name/phone/maybe employer, and the card-on-card question is how the caller buys the missing underwriting data from you.

**Unverified / not found:** HDFC's exact pre-approval criteria for cards; bank-published card-on-card thresholds (limit, vintage, income waiver) at HDFC/Axis/SBI Card/ICICI/IDFC; a TransUnion CIBIL product named "CIBIL Prospect"; CRED/Paytm score-check consent text; whether fintechs cold-call non-users; any RBI text explicitly allowing or barring marketing-only bulk bureau pulls on existing customers.

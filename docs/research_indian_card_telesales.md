# Evidence pack: Indian credit-card telesales calls

Compiled 24 Oct 2026 from public sources (web search + fetch). Used to design the simulated-customer persona bank and the compliance checks. Every claim carries its source URL. Where a number could not be found, it says so. Reddit was blocked to the crawler and most Technofino/Quora pages returned 403, so "colour" quotes come from Jagoinvestor comments, consumer-complaint boards, news reports and search snippets of forum threads.

## 1. How annoyed Indians are

**Volume.** Truecaller's India Insights 2025 (data Jan–Dec 2025): 4,168 crore spam calls identified, 1,189 crore blocked by users, 770 crore classed as fraudulent; average blocked spam call 1.8 minutes (https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/india/story/indians-received-over-4000-crore-spam-calls-in-2025-1189-crore-blocked-report-515765-2026-02-11). Truecaller's 2026 global report: India 5th worst, spam intensity 66% of unknown-number calls; sales/telemarketing 36% of spam, financial services 18%, scams 12% (https://newsarenaindia.com/economy/india-5th-among-most-spam-affected-nations/76485). No 2025 per-user monthly figure is published; the last Truecaller per-user number was 16.8 spam calls/user/month (2021 report) (https://inc42.com/buzz/india-ranks-fourth-in-spam-and-scam-calls-worldwide).

**LocalCircles (Sept 2024, 71,000+ responses, 371 districts):** 95% get pesky calls daily; 18% get 1–2/day, 34% 3–5, 20% 6–10, 23% over 10; 96% of DND-registered users still get them; financial services is the source for 66% (up from 54% in Feb 2024), real estate 22%; 66% of calls come from individual-looking mobile numbers (https://www.localcircles.com/a/press/page/pesky-calls-survey-2024). Feb 2024 wave: among financial-services callers Bajaj Finance 40%, HDFC group 27% (HDFC Life 15%, HDFC Bank 12%), Kotak 4%, ICICI 2% (https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/top-story/story/kya-aapko-loan-chahiye-6-in-10-indians-get-3-or-more-pesky-calls-each-day-reveals-survey-418013-2024-02-19). Nov 2024 wave (14,000 responses): only 9% saw a net reduction after TRAI's blacklisting; 18% said robocalls increased instead (https://www.localcircles.com/a/press/page/trai-spam-call-survey).

**How people react (LocalCircles, May 2022, 9,084 responses):** 42% pick up then block; 21% pick up and tell the caller not to call again; 14% use a caller-ID app and don't pick up; 14% only answer phonebook numbers; 7% do nothing; 2% talk to most callers (https://www.localcircles.com/a/press/page/spam-calls-2022-survey). Truecaller/Tata Tele "State of Business Calling 2026" (Kantar, 1,000 consumers, 500 businesses, 17 cities): 79% say customers avoid unknown business calls, yet 76% of consumers prefer voice for business interactions and 49% would answer more if they could schedule a callback (https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/79-of-indians-avoid-unknown-business-calls-creating-new-challenge-for-brands-report-541499-2026-07-08). Truecaller's CEO posted that over 8 months users ignored 81% of 140-series and 79% of 1600-series calls (https://se.linkedin.com/in/kunaldua).

**TRAI/UCC complaints and enforcement.** 2025: 31.09 lakh UCC complaints (17.06 lakh via DND app); 7,31,120 notices to unregistered telemarketers; 1,84,482 resources disconnected; 21.05 lakh cumulative disconnections since Aug 2024; DND app installs 28.08 lakh (https://www.latestly.com/technology/trai-issues-over-7-31-lakh-notices-to-unregistered-telemarketers-in-2025-disconnects-1-84-lakh-mobile-numbers-to-curb-spam-7313193.html/amp). Apr–Jun 2026: 1.085 million complaints, ~1.5 per million calls; 263 senders blacklisted (https://ianslive.in/govt-takes-action-against-183000-telecom-resources-blacklists-263-senders-in-q1-fy27--20260804154006). TRAI has fined operators Rs 153.8 crore for wrongly closing UCC complaints (https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-08/PR_No111of2026.pdf). 140xx is for promotional, 1600xx for service/transactional calls incl. banking (https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/business/3959342-trai-takes-action-against-183-lakh-telecom-resources-to-curb-unsolicited-commercial-communications). Digital Consent Acquisition: TRAI–RBI pilot live at 11 banks, consent sought via 127xxx short code, pilot due to end Feb 2026 (https://www.medianama.com/2025/10/223-digital-consent-platform-trai-rbi-february-2026/). Not confirmed whether the pilot has closed.

## 2. Behavioural data on cold calls

No Indian time-to-hangup dataset was found; global figures (labelled) below.

- Allo, 72,339 US/Canada cold dials, Jul–Sep 2026: connect rate 26%; of connected calls 9.5% end under 15 s, 34.4% under 30 s, 71% under 60 s; median 40 s; only 7.4% of dials become a 1-minute-plus conversation; best hours 11:00–16:00 local (https://www.withallo.com/blog/cold-calling-statistics).
- Klenty, 12,437 B2B calls: median call 55 s, 53.8% end before 60 s (https://www.klenty.com/blog/how-long-should-a-cold-call-last/). Belkins, 175,000 dials: 58% of connects become a conversation (https://belkins.io/blog/cold-calling-benchmarks).
- Gong, 90,380 calls: "How have you been?" 10.01% success vs 1.5% baseline; "Did I catch you at a bad time?" 40% less likely to book; successful calls average 5:50 vs 3:14 for failures (https://www.scienceofpeople.com/cold-call/). Gong 300M-call study: permission-based opener 11.18% (https://www.gong.io/blog/the-best-and-worst-cold-call-openers-backed-by-data-from-300m-calls).
- B2C outbound conversion benchmarks: 1–3% average, 3–5% good, 5–8% excellent (https://vcc.live/?p=46114).
- India-specific (vendor blogs, unaudited): telecallers make 80–100 attempts/day with 40–55% connecting, i.e. 35–55 live calls (https://rmdialer.com/blog/telecaller-kpi-report-connect-rate-call-duration/); DSA card/loan products need 8–10 touches before a submission (https://callyzer.co/blog/12-touch-rule-why-indian-telecallers-stop-following-up/); an agent dialling 150 numbers/day at ~1% conversion is the stated economics of card telesales (https://www.thecore.in/podcasts/tring-tring-inside-indias-spam-call-economy-859560); open in the prospect's regional language then switch on their reply (https://www.sahay.io/blog/complete-telecalling-guide-india). No credit-card-specific Indian conversion figure was found.
- AI-specific: SquadStack defines "abrupt disconnection rate" as hang-up within 10 s of realising it is AI; its AI fell from ~40% (Jan 2025) to ~10% (Oct 2025) vs 8–12% for humans and >70% for IVR (https://www.squadstack.ai/).

**Verbatim-style "get rid of the caller" patterns (Indian):**
1. "I already have the product they are trying to sell" (https://www.jagoinvestor.com/2013/07/10-tricks-to-avoid-unwanted-marketing-calls.html)
2. "I ask them how they got my number and threaten to complain" (same)
3. "I am a student pursuing MBA and I want the loan/credit card to pay my college fee" (same)
4. "Income is 10k per month and an advocate by profession" (same)
5. "No one with that name works here" / wrong number (same)
6. Speaker on, say "ok" occasionally until they give up (same)
7. "I just lost my job" (same; a Technofino poster says it did not work: https://technofino.in/community/threads/spam-credit-card-related-calls.3547/)
8. "I have to attend, respond and block the number" after daily calls despite bank DND (https://www.consumercomplaints.in/hdfc-bank-receiving-marketing-calls-despite-of-dnd-activated-c3518077)
9. "I have blocked them. But they have several numbers… It's so disruptive" (15–20 calls/day) (https://www.thecore.in/podcasts/tring-tring-inside-indias-spam-call-economy-859560)
10. Declined "4-5 times in a day" then the agent turned rude (https://www.complaintboard.in/complaints-reviews/telecallers-l155050.html)
11. Reprimanding the caller, then getting a call from the "manager" asking why he was rude (https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/latest/trends/story/bengaluru-doctor-slams-hdfc-after-getting-unsolicited-loan-spam-calls-bank-says-sorry-to-hear-about-your-experience-423589-2024-03-31)
12. Texting START DND to 1909 / reporting via TRAI DND app ("spam calls reduced to nil after complaint") (https://www.technofino.in/community/threads/trai-dnd-3-0-app-%E2%80%94-super-easy-way-to-report-spam-calls-sms.40614/)

**Situations where people were happy with a card call:**
1. Unsolicited caller offered Regalia Gold lifetime-free; member doubted it, but ultimately got LTF after escalation (https://technofino.in/community/threads/need-clarification-hdfc-regalia-gold-offered-as-lifetime-free-ltf-%E2%80%93-genuine-or-sales-gimmick.44901/)
2. Diners Club Black upgrade offer accepted because first year was free (https://www.technofino.in/community/threads/got-an-upgrade-for-hdfc-dcb-diners-club-black.7746/)
3. Infinia/DCB upgrades obtained once the member asked for the upgrade form (https://technofino.in/community/threads/how-to-get-hdfc-bank-infinia-dcb-credit-card-easily-new-upgrade.703/)
4. Consumers say they would answer if they could schedule a callback (49%) and 76% prefer voice for business matters (https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/79-of-indians-avoid-unknown-business-calls-creating-new-challenge-for-brands-report-541499-2026-07-08)
5. Pre-approved offers visible in the app/net banking are what members tell each other to check before accepting (https://technofino.in/community/threads/need-clarification-hdfc-regalia-gold-offered-as-lifetime-free-ltf-%E2%80%93-genuine-or-sales-gimmick.44901/)

## 3. Regulation

**Important change:** the 2022 Master Direction has been replaced by the Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks – Credit Cards and Debit Cards: Issuance and Conduct) Directions, 2025, RBI/DOR/2025-26/155, dated 28 Nov 2025, effective immediately; para 93 repeals earlier instructions (https://taxguru.in/?p=1005490; RBI master-direction page, updated 1 Oct 2026: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=13155). RBI's PDF is captcha-gated, so paragraph numbers below are from TaxGuru's reproduction, cross-checked against the identically numbered RRB edition (https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13052) and the 2022 text (https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12300).

- Calling hours: para 11(9) "The card-issuer's representatives shall contact the customers only between 10:00 hrs and 19:00 hrs"; telemarketers must comply with TRAI regulations and NCPR (2022: para 6(a)(ix)). Confirmed.
- Unsolicited cards: para 11(4) "The issue of unsolicited cards/upgradation is strictly prohibited"; if billed, reverse charges and pay "twice the value of the charges reversed"; close within 7 working days if no consent (2022: 6(a)(iv)).
- Consent: para 11(8) written consent before issuing, or digital mode with multifactor authentication; 11(6) OTP consent to activate a card unused for 30 days; 12(5) explicit consent for overlimit.
- KFS/MITC: 11(1) one-page Key Fact Statement with the application; 11(2) MITC in welcome kit and on changes; para 91 contents, para 92 Arial-12 minimum.
- Agents: para 44 "card-issuers shall also be liable for the acts of their agents", act on repeated mis-selling incl. blacklisting; para 43 random checks and mystery shopping; 11(10) DSAs/DMAs only solicit/service, decision stays with issuer.
- Upgrades: para 34 no unilateral upgrade or credit-limit enhancement.
- Call recording: no requirement in the Directions; recording appears only as a permitted activation channel in the explanation to 11(6).
- 2026 amendment (RBI/2026-27/29, 27 Apr 2026): para 23(5) "past due" reporting/penal charges only after 3 days, effective 1 Apr 2027 (https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13388).

**TRAI TCCCPR 2018.** The TRAI server refused connections, so the primary PDF could not be quoted. Preference categories include "Banking/Insurance/Financial products/credit cards"; subscriber-selectable modes include robo/auto-dialler calls and time bands (https://trai.gov.in/preference-registration). Secondary sources state promotional calls are limited to 09:00–21:00 IST, transactional unrestricted (https://developer.exotel.com/docs/faqs/calling-hours). RBI's 10:00–19:00 is therefore the binding window. Second Amendment (12 Feb 2025): action after 5 complaints in 10 days (was 10 in 7); complaint window 7 days (was 3); inferred consent lapses with the contract; explicit transactional consent valid 7 days; access-provider penalties Rs 2/5/10 lakh (https://moneylife.in/article/spam-calls-sms-regulations-tightened-by-trai-5-complaints-to-trigger-action-against-spammer/76368.html; https://mondaq.com/india/telecoms-mobile-cable-communications/1607946/efforts-to-curb-spam-continue-trai-releases-second-amendment-to-the-tcccpr-2018; https://securiti.ai/india-spam-rules-trai-latest-amendment/). Third Amendment (05 of 2026, notified 18 Sep 2026): Reg 21A AI/ML flagging of suspect CLIs shared within 2 hours; 5 flagged CLIs in 10 days triggers KYC then physical verification, 15-day outgoing bar; A2P/automated calls must be pre-declared or are treated as UCC; terminating operator may charge up to Rs 0.05/min; call-blocking apps may not tag 140/1600 series; Rs 1,000 per valid complaint for scrubbing failures; "BLOCK PROMO" preference; staged commencement 30/60/90 days after gazette (https://foxmandal.in/?p=60468; https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-09/PR_No119of2026.pdf).

## 4. Fraud patterns to be distinguishable from

- RBI Annual Report FY2024-25: 13,516 card/internet frauds, 56.5% of 23,953 reported frauds (Rs 1 lakh+) (https://www.medianama.com/2025/12/223-cards-internet-fraud-66-in-fy25-decline-banking-rbi/). I4C 2025: Rs 19,812 crore lost across 21.8 lakh complaints, 7% via credit-card fraud (https://ianslive.in/indians-lose-over-rs-52976-crore-to-cyber-frauds-over-six-years-report--20260103154943). No vishing-specific count was found.
- "Limit upgrade" OTP scam: caller offers a higher limit, the OTP actually authorises a transfer (https://www.business-standard.com/amp/finance/personal-finance/falling-for-a-card-limit-upgrade-that-otp-could-drain-your-account-125102800527_1.html); Bengaluru victim lost Rs 2.94 lakh via "protection plan upgrade" link (https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fkarnataka%2Fbengaluru%2Fotp-scam-college-student-loses-rs-294-lakh-to-online-fraud-3488923); Kaithal case: OTP, then PAN/card photos for "KYC", then screen-share (https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/kaithal-cops-crack-down-on-cybercriminals-11-held-for-credit-card-fraud); Mumbai: 19 victims, Rs 8 lakh, spoofed caller ID (https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/personal-finance/banking/story/credit-card-frauds-mumbai-rise-8-lakh-losses-rbi-liability-guidelines-484917-2025-07-16).
- Bank/regulator rules a legitimate agent must satisfy: HDFC "will never ask for your OTP, PIN, password, CVV or full card details over the phone" and its genuine calls come from 1600/140 series (https://www.zeebiz.com/personal-finance/banking/news-attention-hdfc-bank-customers-if-it-s-not-1600-or-140-private-lender-issues-fraud-warning-396253); RBL says the same about 1600/140 (https://www.rbl.bank.in/customer-knowledge-center/beware-of-scam-calls); RBI: verify caller identity, never share OTP/CVV/PIN, report at 1930 (https://website.rbi.org.in/web/rbi/rbi-kehta-hai/fraud-emails-calls-and-smses).
- Typical sceptical challenges: "how did you get my number" (https://www.jagoinvestor.com/2013/07/10-tricks-to-avoid-unwanted-marketing-calls.html); "is this genuine or a sales gimmick / show it in the app" (https://technofino.in/community/threads/need-clarification-hdfc-regalia-gold-offered-as-lifetime-free-ltf-%E2%80%93-genuine-or-sales-gimmick.44901/); "I'll verify independently and call the bank on the number on my card" (https://www.publicservicesmap.in/cyber-safety/fake-bank-call); any urgency ("card used fraudulently, account blocked") is treated as bogus (https://www.india.com/technology/got-a-strange-voicemail-claiming-to-be-rbi-the-hidden-truth-pib-just-exposed-will-shock-you-8193662/); "send details by mail" as a brush-off (https://callyzer.co/blog/how-to-effectively-sell-your-product-or-service-as-a-telecaller-in-india/). Credit-card complaints to the RBI Ombudsman rose 20% to 50,811 in FY25 (https://www.medianama.com/2025/12/223-rbi-ombudsman-14-banking-complaints-fy25-private-banks/).

## Implications for simulated-customer design

**Suggested persona mix (justified):**
- 35% Instant rejecter (hangs up or says "not interested" within 15 s): LocalCircles 42% pick-and-block + 21% tell-not-to-call; 34% of connects end under 30 s.
- 20% Polite brush-off (excuse: already have card, student, low income, "send email"): Jagoinvestor/Technofino patterns.
- 15% Sceptic/fraud-tester (asks number source, demands 1600/140 CLI, app proof, refuses OTP, will call bank back): HDFC/RBI advisories, upgrade-scam prevalence.
- 10% Angry/complainer (abusive, threatens DND/1909 complaint, "fifth call today"): 23% get 10+ calls/day; 31 lakh complaints in 2025.
- 10% Warm prospect (pre-approved, checks fee/LTF in writing, regional-language preference): Technofino upgrade threads; 76% prefer voice.
- 5% Busy/deferrer ("call later", asks callback slot): 49% would answer if callback schedulable.
- 5% Confused/vulnerable (elderly, language mismatch, asks "which bank?", may over-share): fraud case profiles.

**Top 10 hangup triggers, ranked by evidence strength:**
1. Not recognising caller / unknown 10-digit number (79% avoid; 81% ignore 140-series).
2. Fifth-plus call of the day / DND already registered (95–96% of DND users still called).
3. Opener asks "bad time?" or fails to state reason within 10 s (Gong 40% penalty; 34% of connects gone by 30 s).
4. Realising it is an AI/IVR voice (ADR 40%→10% for AI, >70% IVR).
5. Any request for OTP/CVV/PAN/link/APK (bank rules, scam coverage).
6. Call outside 10:00–19:00 (RBI para 11(9); also strongest compliance tripwire).
7. Pitch after an explicit "no" or request to be removed (complaint-board rudeness reports; RBI 11(4) unsolicited rule).
8. "Lifetime free"/upgrade claims without in-app or written confirmation (Technofino scepticism).
9. Wrong language or no regional-language option (Indian telecalling guides; Sarvam's 12-language scale).
10. Urgency or threat framing ("card blocked", "last chance") (PIB fact-check, RBI advisories); weakest direct evidence for sales calls, strong for fraud.
